# Eastern campus footprint notes

- Source: official Southeast University Jiulonghu campus masterplan supplied as `seu-jiulonghu-official-masterplan-2025.jpg`, 1375 × 867 pixels. Coordinates are unscaled original-image pixels, x rightward and y downward.
- 212 editable footprint components. Existing slate-gray roofs: 153; orange under-construction roofs: 20; light-gray planned roofs: 39.
- Main coverage: east of x=650, plus full P8 footprint across the boundary. Undergraduate teaching A1 is excluded because it is represented in the western/central data.
- 34 older dormitory courtyards are represented with four separate footprint wings each, keeping their centers open. Main F3 narrow green courts are also kept open.
- No open M4 running track, playing field, or tennis/basketball court is extruded. M3 northeast is interpreted as a small planned covered sports structure; its roof edges are low confidence due to label occlusion.
- M2 swimming hall height 11.05m is officially reported: https://news.seu.edu.cn/2019/0430/c5486a271243/page.htm . All other heights are estimated massing values, not measured building heights.
- E3/E4/E5 were changed to existing per user confirmation on2026-10-16, while original plan statuses remain in plan_status_2025. Separate current-status metadata records the university report that Lanyuan opened in August 2025: https://xxgk.seu.edu.cn/2025/0825/c10804a537033/page.htm . Contractor reporting gives five buildings with a maximum of 18 above-ground floors; individual buildings could not be matched to exact heights, so E3 pieces keep a clearly marked24m height placeholder.
- The planned faculty plot immediately east of the gym reads approximately P11 in the raster; the south one reads P18. P11 should be checked against a higher-resolution source before treating its number as definitive.
- Q, E4/I, E5/G6, P9, and P8 wings are simplified; roof-shadow and label occlusion can cause a few pixels of edge uncertainty.
- Footprint overlay visually checked against the reference. All polygons have positive nonzero area and no strict self-intersection.
